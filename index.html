Molecular Motion Sandbox

Explore how particles move, attract, and trade energy they change state of matter.

State: Solid Energy level

Melting

Solid → Liquid as particles gain energy and loosen their structure.

Freezing

Liquid → Solid as particles lose energy and lock into place.

Vaporization

Liquid → Gas as particles escape attractive forces.

Condensation

Gas → Liquid as particles slow down and cluster.

Sublimation

Solid → Gas with a direct leap in energy.

Deposition

Gas → Solid as energy is rapidly removed.

Ionization

Gas → Plasma as particles are stripped into charged ions.

Recombination

Plasma → Gas as ions recapture electrons.
